Privacy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Ad Tray does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. I receive nothing from your copy of the extension.

What is stored, and where

The following is kept in chrome.storage.local on your own device, and nowhere else:

  • The pop-ups currently held in the tray, including their URL and hostname
  • Your trusted-site list
  • Any always-hold rules you create
  • Whether the tray is visible or minimised
  • A short local log of recent decisions, so you can see why a pop-up was held or allowed

Removing the extension deletes all of it. You can clear the trusted-site list at any time from the extension’s settings.

Network requests

The extension makes no outbound requests of its own. An optional reputation check against Google Safe Browsing exists, but it is inactive unless you enter your own API key in the settings. With no key, no request is made and held pop-ups are simply labelled unknown.

Third-party pages in the tray

Held pop-ups render inside sandboxed frames. Those are real websites and may set cookies or collect data under their own policies, exactly as they would have if the pop-up had opened as a tab. I have no access to and no control over what they do. The sandbox does prevent them from opening further pop-ups, navigating the page you are on, or reaching their own origin’s cookies and storage.

Permissions

Ad Tray requests access to all sites because a pop-up can come from any site. The interceptor stays inert during normal browsing and acts only when a page tries to open a new window. It does not read page content, form fields, or credentials.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. If a future version ever does send data anywhere, that will be stated here plainly and asked for explicitly, not buried.
